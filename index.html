I am a Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) in Public Policy at the Department of Political Economy, King's College London. My research sits at the intersection of political economy and comparative public policy. I am interested in tax and climate policy as well as in the political consequences of technological change.

I received my PhD in Political and Social Sciences from the European University Institute (2019) and held a postdoctoral position at LMU Munich before joining KCL.
